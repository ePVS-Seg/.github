# ePVS-Seg

**Enlarged Perivascular Space Segmentation**

Welcome to the **ePVS-Seg** organization — a collaborative research initiative focused on the automated detection and segmentation of enlarged perivascular spaces (ePVS) in brain MRI.

---

## 🧠 About

Perivascular spaces (PVS) are fluid-filled spaces surrounding blood vessels in the brain. When enlarged, they serve as imaging biomarkers associated with several neurological conditions, including:

- Small vessel disease (SVD)
- Neuroinflammation
- Neurodegenerative disorders (e.g., Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease)
- Sleep and glymphatic dysfunction

The goal of this organization is to develop, benchmark, and share open-source tools for the automated segmentation of ePVS from structural MRI data — enabling reproducible, scalable research across large neuroimaging cohorts.

---

## 🔬 Research Focus

- **Automated segmentation** of ePVS in T1w, T2w, and multi-modal MRI
- **Deep learning architectures** tailored for small-structure detection (e.g., U-Net variants, attention mechanisms)
- **Benchmark datasets and evaluation metrics** for ePVS segmentation
- **Clinical translation** — supporting large-scale studies and population-based neuroimaging

---

## 🛠️ Technologies & Tools

- Python · PyTorch · TensorFlow
- Medical image processing (ANTs, FSL, SimpleITK)
- BIDS-compatible neuroimaging workflows
- Reproducible research with containerized environments (Docker, Singularity)
